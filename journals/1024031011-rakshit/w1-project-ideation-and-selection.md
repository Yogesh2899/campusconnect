# Week 1 - Project ideation and selection

## Problem

Identify what users need from the interface when arranging consultations.

## Relevant Context

Students and faculty relied on messages or email to arrange consultations, which made available times difficult to track.

## Key Observation

Students and professors work toward different goals. I organized the proposed navigation around the actions each role needs most often.

## Solution

I reviewed how students and professors coordinate meetings by email and informal messages. I mapped the key steps to proposed interface areas: a professor availability view, a booking flow and a meeting history view for students, and an availability editor and request list for professors. The first interface scope covered availability, requests and meeting status.

## Next Steps

Turn the main scheduling problems into a list of required screens.
