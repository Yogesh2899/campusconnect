# Week 1 - Project ideation and selection

## Problem

Identify the core backend responsibilities and assess initial feasibility.

## Relevant Context

Students and faculty relied on messages or email to arrange consultations, which made available times difficult to track.

## Key Observation

The project scope included several possible extensions. I focused the initial service design on account access, availability and the meeting lifecycle.

## Solution

I helped evaluate the project scope and feature list. I identified the records that the system would need to keep for accounts, faculty availability, appointments and notifications. I used those records to outline the service responsibilities behind viewing slots and requesting consultations. The project had a preliminary backend scope and a list of core data needs.

## Next Steps

Organize the data and service responsibilities into backend modules.
