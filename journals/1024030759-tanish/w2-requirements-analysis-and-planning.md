# Week 2 - Requirements analysis and planning

## Problem

Plan backend modules and record the system's quality requirements.

## Relevant Context

The selected project needed a clear boundary across account access, faculty availability, appointment requests and updates.

## Key Observation

Booking crosses several modules. I treated availability validation and meeting persistence as related operations so that the booking design accounts for concurrent requests.

## Solution

I worked on feasibility, quality requirements and the rationale for Agile Scrum. I mapped the initial modules to authentication, availability, scheduling and notifications. I identified data integrity and conflict prevention as important requirements for the scheduling service. The planning work defined backend responsibilities and the requirements that guide their design.

## Next Steps

Convert the requirements into service inputs, preconditions and outcomes.
