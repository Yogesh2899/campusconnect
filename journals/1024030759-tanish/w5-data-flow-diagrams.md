# Week 5 - Data flow diagrams

## Problem

Document processes and data stores in the DFDs.

## Relevant Context

The team used three levels of data flow diagrams to connect external interactions to internal processes and records.

## Key Observation

A decomposed process must keep the same boundary flows as its parent. I checked the external inputs and outputs while adding the internal steps.

## Solution

I focused on authentication, notifications and their detailed data flows. The Level 1 design connects the main processes to stores for users, availability, meeting records and notifications. At Level 2, I reviewed how credentials, sessions, slot checks and meeting updates fit into the major processes. The DFDs documented the backend processes, data stores and information exchanged.

## Next Steps

Document the proposed technology and data integrity constraints in the SRS.
