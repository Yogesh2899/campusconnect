# Week 7 - User stories and acceptance criteria

## Problem

Define expected service outcomes for notification and administration stories.

## Relevant Context

The story cards made the requirements observable through acceptance criteria tied to individual roles.

## Key Observation

Similar notifications may represent different events. I checked that request creation, approval, rejection, rescheduling and cancellation each had a clear trigger.

## Solution

I worked on notification and administration story cards and reviewed their consistency with the SRS. For meeting updates, I traced the event, intended recipient and related appointment record. For administrative functions, I considered the authorized actor and the data required for reporting or configuration. The story cards provided expected outcomes for service integration and future verification.

## Next Steps

Check service responsibilities and data stores against the consolidated report.
