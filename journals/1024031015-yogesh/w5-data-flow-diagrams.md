# Week 5 - Data flow diagrams

## Problem

Review the system boundary and the flow of account and meeting data.

## Relevant Context

The team used three levels of data flow diagrams to connect external interactions to internal processes and records.

## Key Observation

The context diagram cannot show every internal control. I used the detailed flows to connect security checks to the processes responsible for enforcing them.

## Solution

I focused on the context-level DFD and student-related flows, then reviewed how the detailed diagrams handled credentials, sessions, availability checks and meeting records. I identified where authentication and authorization should be verified and where the booking process must protect scheduling data from inconsistent updates. The DFD review linked proposed controls to authentication and scheduling processes.

## Next Steps

Tie the proposed security checks to the SRS.
