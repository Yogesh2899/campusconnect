# Week 1 - Project ideation and selection

## Problem

Define the scheduling problem and identify situations the project needs to handle.

## Relevant Context

Students and faculty relied on messages or email to arrange consultations, which made available times difficult to track.

## Key Observation

A slot can become unavailable after a student views it. I treated availability viewing and booking confirmation as distinct checks.

## Solution

I helped evaluate the project idea and outline the proposed solution. I focused the problem statement on unclear availability, delayed replies and overlapping consultations. From the student workflow, I identified three core scenarios for verification: finding a valid slot, requesting it and following the resulting meeting status. The initial project scope and a starting set of scheduling scenarios were defined.

## Next Steps

Identify the main functional and access-control requirements.
