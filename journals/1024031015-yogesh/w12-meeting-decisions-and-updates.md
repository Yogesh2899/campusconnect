# Week 12 - Meeting decisions and updates

## Problem

A request created by a student must carry through to the assigned professor and back to the student.

## Relevant Context

A request progresses from Pending through a decision or a follow-up action such as cancellation or rescheduling.

## Key Observation

A professor’s decision must be tied to the correct request and the affected participant.

## Solution

I traced the expected outcomes for pending, approved, rejected, rescheduled and cancelled meetings.

## Next Steps

Compare what both roles see after a decision.
